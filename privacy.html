The complete answer: The Compress research prototype processes files on your local machine. No data is transmitted to LPagesAppLabs or any third party. No analytics. No telemetry. No accounts. The marketing website also collects nothing.

01 Scope

This policy covers the compress.lpagesapplabs.com marketing website and the Compress (.apx) research prototype tool, in its current and future versions.

02 Marketing Website

  • No cookies

03 Research Prototype Tool

The Compress prototype is a local Python (and planned Rust) tool:

  • Processes files on your local filesystem only
  • Makes no network calls
  • Sends no telemetry or analytics
  • Requires no account or login

If future versions add any network features (e.g. cloud metadata indexing), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the feature will be opt-in.
